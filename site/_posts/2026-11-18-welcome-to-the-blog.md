This blog is where we share engineering notes and project updates alongside the structured documentation.

## What you'll find here

- **Engineering notes**: technical decisions, trade-offs, and lessons learned while implementing the RPG Combat kata.
- **Software factory notes**: experiments with coding agents, skills, and workflows.
- **Project updates**: progress through the user stories and changes to the project.

## How posts are organised

Posts are grouped by **category** (broad topic area) and **tag** (specific concept). Use the chips on the blog page and on each post to browse by either dimension.

## Authoring a new post

Add a file to `site/_posts/` following the `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` naming convention with this front matter:

```yaml
---
title: "Your Post Title"
author: "Your Name"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
categories: [category-name]
tags: [tag1, tag2]
---
```

The post appears in the blog listing and in the matching tag and category archive pages.
